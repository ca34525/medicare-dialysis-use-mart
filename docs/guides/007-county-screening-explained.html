The purpose

A screen should show its work

Plan 007 puts two already-governed county facts side by side. The Medicare component is observed outpatient dialysis use among Original Medicare beneficiaries. The social component is CDC/ATSDR's overall SVI county percentile. The result is an investigation aid, not a clinical conclusion.

Think of graph paper

Draw one vertical line and one horizontal line. Every county with both coordinates lands in one of four visible boxes. If either coordinate is missing, leave the point off the graph and say why.

No secret recipe

There is no weighting, score, model, or ranking. The contract identity county_screening.v1 records the two named rules.

One declared grain

“Grain” means what one row represents: one candidate build × latest CMS year × current reconciled five-character county FIPS.

Input to output

Five guarded steps make the classification

01Prove one run

Accept exactly one paired CMS/SVI input audit and its immutable lineage.

02Select candidates

Keep the latest governed CMS year and current counties that passed Plan 006 reconciliation.

03Freeze P75

Calculate the national continuous county percentile once from eligible Medicare values.

04Classify rows

Apply both inclusive boundaries, or preserve a precise insufficient-data state.

05Reconcile totals

Emit all five categories, even when one has a count of zero, and prove they sum to the candidates.

Lineage is the receipt trail from an output back to its exact inputs. Every threshold, county row, and audit total carries the paired input-set hash and both source identities.

The two components

Both boundaries are inclusive and fixed

Medicare-use boundary

  • Field: BENES_OP_DLYS_PCT.
  • Denominator: the CMS source-defined Original Medicare beneficiary denominator.
  • Population: latest-year, current, reported, nonnull county values nationally.
  • Method: continuous P75, continuous_linear_type_7.
  • Rule: value at or above P75 is higher use.

Social-vulnerability boundary

  • Field: RPL_THEMES.
  • Vintage: static CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022.
  • Scale: U.S.-based county percentile rank from 0 to 1.
  • Boundary: exactly 0.75.
  • Rule: value at or above 0.75 is higher SVI.
What “continuous” means: the percentile may fall between two observed county values. In the deterministic four-value fixture—0.0100000000, 0.0200000000, 0.0300000000, and 0.0400000000—P75 is 0.0325000000. Equality is deliberately placed on the higher side, so tied boundary rows behave consistently.

The national P75 is calculated once and copied unchanged onto every row. It is not recalculated for a state or dashboard filter, and it is not the source-published CMS National benchmark. Reported numeric zero remains eligible; suppression, blanks, NA, unavailable values, and nulls never become zero.

Evidence and failure behavior

The mart fails closed when its story cannot reconcile

T-016 · threshold

Proves the national P75 uses only latest, current, reported, nonnull county values and remains fixed across the mart.

T-017 · categories

Proves the four complete quadrants plus insufficient data equal the full candidate county count.

T-020 · lineage

Proves each published metric retains grain, denominator, vintage, and source lineage.

Publication-blocking examples: two “latest” years, historical-county leakage, duplicate run audits, a source-lineage mismatch, a threshold outside 0–1, threshold drift between county rows, a component flag that disagrees with its value, or an audit total that does not reconcile.

Reproducibility evidence from 2026-08-15 UTC

Two independent builds from the same pinned input set reproduced P75 0.0086000000, all five counts, and the same ordered semantic SHA-256 values. The threshold, county mart, and audit summary hashes begin 3df5e14c, 5fffc53a, and 1796d8d4. These are dated snapshot results, not timeless county-count promises.

A separate deterministic fixture reproduced P75 0.0325000000 and exercises reported zero, equality on both boundaries, every quadrant, every insufficient-data reason, and named failure injections without network access.

Safety boundaries

What the screen does not say or do

Completed in Plan 007

  • Fixed and governed national P75.
  • Inclusive Medicare and SVI component flags.
  • One transparent category per current county.
  • Explicit missingness and five-category reconciliation.
  • Run and source lineage on every output.

Deferred or prohibited

  • Facility ingestion, geography assignment, and due-diligence context.
  • Parquet publication, Airflow, Power BI, hosting, and optional AWS.
  • Patient-level data, PHI, clinical or causal claims.
  • Opaque scores, automated decisions, provider rankings, or final site-selection, partnership, or contracting recommendations.

Facility rule: future facility characteristics may help an analyst investigate a county, but they can never change its screening quadrant.

Short glossary

Candidate
A current, reconciled county row eligible to appear in the screen, even when a component is unavailable.
Denominator
The population used under a measure; the CMS percentage retains its source-defined Original Medicare beneficiary denominator.
Percentile
A boundary describing position within an ordered population; P75 marks the 75th-percentile boundary.
Quadrant
One of four boxes formed by crossing two higher/lower component flags.
Lineage
The identities and hashes that trace a result back to the exact governed inputs.
