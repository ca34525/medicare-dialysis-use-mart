What is a source contract?

A source contract is a tested agreement about incoming data. For cdc_svi_county_2022, it says exactly which official ArcGIS layer is allowed, which fields must exist, what types those fields use, and what makes one county row identifiable.

✓
Think of a receiving checklist. A warehouse does not accept a delivery merely because the truck says “SVI.” It checks the shipment number, labels, quantities, and seals first. The code does the same before later work may paginate, type, or join the data.

This protects the product from a subtle but serious error: accidentally using a tract layer, state-based rankings, numeric-coerced FIPS, or a changed field while producing results that still look plausible.

What the contract requires

The layer contains 161 fields. Seventeen are required now; the remaining 144 are allowed additions and are reported rather than rejected. The required fields fall into four jobs.

JobRequired fieldsWhy they matter
County identityST, STATE, ST_ABBR, STCNTY, COUNTYPreserve a traceable five-character county FIPS and source labels.
Transport orderingGRASP_IDOrder later API pages deterministically. It is not the business key.
SVI ranksRPL_THEMES, RPL_THEME1 through RPL_THEME4Retain the overall and four U.S.-based county percentile ranks.
Plain-language contextEP_POV150, EP_UNINSUR, EP_AGE65, EP_DISABL, EP_LIMENG, EP_NOVEHKeep a small, interpretable set of source percentages with their own denominators.

RPL_THEMES is the overall U.S.-based county percentile rank. The later screening mart will classify a valid value at or above 0.75 as high social vulnerability. This contract only proves the source meaning and raw availability; it does not perform that classification.

Safety rule: these percentages are descriptive context. They are not summed, averaged into a score, or allowed to change the screening quadrant.

Why -999 is not zero

0

A reported numeric zero remains a real zero. The fixture preserves it exactly.

-999

The official documentation says the value was unavailable or could not be calculated because Census data were unavailable.

Plan 004 deliberately leaves both values raw. A later staging plan must convert -999 to null with an explicit unavailable status, keep zero as zero, and reject any non-null rank outside [0,1]. The fixture already contains 0, 0.75, 1, -999, and an out-of-range value so those future rules can begin with deterministic evidence.

What fails, and what waits

Contract-blocking failures

  • Wrong layer ID or a tract-like layer name.
  • Missing, duplicated, or incompatibly typed required field.
  • Object-ID field other than GRASP_ID.
  • Numeric, malformed, mismatched, duplicate, or territory county FIPS.
  • District of Columbia represented by anything other than 11001.

Intentionally deferred

  • Production pagination and immutable raw manifests.
  • Typing and -999 normalization.
  • fct_svi_county and dim_county.
  • CMS-to-SVI reconciliation and the screening quadrant.
  • Facility data, geocoding, Airflow, Parquet, and Power BI.

Interpretation boundary: SVI is static 2022 context based on 2018-2022 ACS data. It is not a 2024 or 2026 observation, a clinical measure, a causal explanation, kidney disease prevalence, unmet need, disease burden, an intervention opportunity, or a final site-selection recommendation.

Short glossary
ACS
American Community Survey, the Census Bureau source underlying the 2018-2022 context.
ArcGIS layer
A named table-and-map resource exposed by the official public service. This contract uses county layer 1 without geometry.
FIPS
A standardized geographic code. County keys here are exactly five text characters.
Grain
What one row represents: one county FIPS in the 2022 U.S. county layer.
Percentile rank
A relative position from 0 to 1 among U.S. counties, not a raw percentage of residents.
Source contract
Executable rules that block changed or malformed source data before downstream use.
Static vintage
A fixed historical context. SVI 2022 is not relabeled as the date when the mart runs.
